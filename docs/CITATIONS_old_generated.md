## Hazard and model parameters

| Parameter | Value | Unit | Grounding | Sources | What resolves it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wave decay rate in sheltered water (λ_W) | 0.35 | per km | **placeholder_needs_calibration** | – | Exponential decay per km of travel through bay/channel water. 0.35/km halves wave intensity in about 2 km. Resolve with a nearshore wave model (e.g. SWAN) run for the Mandovi mouth and Aguada Bay. |
| Surge-level decay rate along the channel (λ_L) | 0.03 | per km | **placeholder_needs_calibration** | Sundar & Shetye 2005; Shetye et al. 2007 | Tides and surge reach far up the Mandovi (tidal influence extends well beyond this window; see Shetye et al. 2007), so the decay is kept small. The prior project's per-hop constants (0.82/hop in channel, 0.6/hop overland) were game values for 100 m hexes: at real scale they would kill the surge within about 1 km of the mouth, so they are NOT used. Resolve with tide-gauge records or a calibrated hydrodynamic model. Supporting evidence: tide-pole records along both estuaries show tidal range INCREASING by about 20% up to ~45 km from the mouth before dropping sharply near the upstream end (Sundar & Shetye 2005). Panjim sits roughly 10–13 km from the Mandovi mouth, well inside that amplifying reach. A surge is also a long wave, so a near-zero decay is the right direction of simplification; if anything the real behaviour here is mild amplification, which a decay rate cannot represent. The tag stays placeholder: the tidal finding is an analogy for surge, not a surge measurement. |
| Fluvial flood decay rate along the channel (λ_F) | 0.05 | per km | **placeholder_needs_calibration** | – | Represents attenuation of the flood wave as the estuary widens towards the sea. Resolve with gauge records or a 1D/2D hydraulic model of the lower Mandovi. |
| Compound water-level ceiling | 3 | × reference | **placeholder_needs_calibration** | Wahl et al. 2015; Moftakhari et al. 2017 | Carried from the prior project. The cited papers establish that surge and fluvial flooding compound; they do not give a 3× ceiling. Resolve with a joint-probability analysis of local surge and river levels. |
| Share of surge-level in the hazard index (α) | 0.5 | fraction | **placeholder_needs_calibration** | – | How much still-water level contributes to the combined hazard index, relative to waves (1 − α). Stands in for the ratio of surge to wave setup/run-up in the reference event's total water level. Resolve with local design-storm surge and wave values. |
| Reference event surge height at the open coast | 1.5 | m | **placeholder_needs_calibration** | Ramakrishnan et al. 2022 | Used only to convert mangrove surge attenuation (cm/km) into index units. No sourced Goa design-surge figure is in this repository yet (the IITM Arabian Sea cyclone assessment in the project folder is a scanned PDF and was not machine-readable). Resolve with an IMD/INCOIS storm-surge estimate for the Goa coast. Analog evidence (not a Goa design value): during Cyclone Tauktae (2021) at Chellanam, Kerala, infragravity waves plus wave setup raised coastal water elevation to about 1.5 m, while the forecast storm surge itself was only about 0.15 m (Ramakrishnan et al. 2022). So 1.5 m is of the right order for an extreme-event coastal water level on this coast, but it is a wave-driven total water elevation, not a surge height; in this tool the value therefore stands in for total still-water elevation, which is one more reason it stays a placeholder until an IMD/INCOIS design-storm study for the Mandovi mouth exists. |
| Reference excess flood depth over low-lying land | 0.5 | m | **placeholder_needs_calibration** | – | Converts the fluvial index into a floodwater volume on each segment's low-lying land (mangrove + khazan + pond cells), which storage solutions then draw down. | High-tide reference level added to every water level | 0 | m above EGM2008 | **placeholder_needs_calibration** | – | Default 0 means the bathtub fill starts from mean sea level. The Mandovi has a mixed, semi-diurnal tide, so a spring high tide sits well above MSL. Enter a local MHWS level relative to the EGM2008 geoid, from the Mormugao tide gauge or a survey benchmark, to include it. No sourced value is in the tool yet. |
| Reference river-flood stage at the upstream edge | 1.0 | m above water level | **placeholder_needs_calibration** | – | Converts the fluvial index F into a water-level rise for the on-map flood extent (level = F × this value).
